Built by a kid with his dad.

Hey, I'm Duncan. I built Drakon.

I'm a kid running real businesses from my house in Texas — including a vendor access site called CertifiedSells. My dad got me into coding, and we've been building stuff together in our garage ever since.

Here's why Drakon exists:

I tried to start an online store more times than I can count. Every time I'd open Shopify, freeze up trying to figure out what to sell, what to post, how to write an ad, how to talk to customers. There were tools out there, but they all felt built for people who already knew what they were doing. Nobody was building for the kid (or the adult) staring at a blank screen trying to figure out where to start.

So I built it. Six AI dragons. Each one handles a different part of running a store. They're for the version of me from a year ago — and for anyone else trying to start their first online business without losing their mind.

I'm building this in my garage with my dad. No funding. No team. Just a kid and his dad who got tired of watching people get stuck — and decided to build the tool we wished existed.
